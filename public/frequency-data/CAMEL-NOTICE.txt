CAMeL Arabic Frequency Lists — Attribution & License
======================================================

This app bundles the Modern Standard Arabic (MSA) frequency list from:

  CAMeL Arabic Frequency Lists
  https://github.com/CAMeL-Lab/Camel_Arabic_Frequency_Lists

Citation:
  Khalifa, Salam and Inoue, Go and Alhafni, Bashar and Baimukan, Nurpeiis
  and Bouamor, Houda and Habash, Nizar. "Camel Arabic Frequency Lists."
  2021. https://github.com/CAMeL-Lab/Camel_Arabic_Frequency_Lists

Associated paper:
  Inoue, Go et al. "The Interplay of Variant, Size, and Task Type in
  Arabic Pre-training." WANLP 2021. https://aclanthology.org/2021.wanlp-1.10/

Data derivation: word types + raw frequency counts extracted from the
same corpora used to pretrain the CAMeLBERT-MSA model (Arabic Gigaword,
Abu El-Khair Corpus, OSIAN, Arabic Wikipedia, and OSCAR), 11.4M unique
word types drawn from 12.6B word tokens. Digits, punctuation, and
non-Arabic-script tokens were excluded by the original authors. Entries
are pre-sorted by descending frequency; this app uses that ordering
directly as each word's frequency rank.
