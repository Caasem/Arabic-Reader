يَوْمٌ طَوِيل

كَانَ الْيَوْمُ طَوِيلًا.

اِسْتَيْقَظَ سَالِمٌ فِي الصَّبَاحِ.

شَرِبَ الْحَلِيبَ وَأَكَلَ الْخُبْزَ.

ذَهَبَ إِلَى الْمَدْرَسَةِ مَعَ أَخِيهِ.

مُلاحَظَة · Note: The reader's dock holds Contents, Search, Marks, Words, Display, Timer and Focus. Contents lists the chapters. Search looks in this book, your library or the dictionary. Display changes the font, size and colours. Timer is a study timer. Focus hides everything but the text; press Esc to leave it.

مُهِمَّة ٥ · Task 5: Open the Ḥāshiya, the margins beside the page where your notes live. Press Alt+M (or use Ḥāshiya in the dock) to hide and show them. In the margin, double-click empty space and type a note. Alt+X captures a region of the page. D opens the whole desk as a document.

بَعْدَ الظُّهْرِ رَجَعَ إِلَى الْبَيْتِ.

كَتَبَ الْوَاجِبَ ثُمَّ رَسَمَ صُورَةً.

رَسَمَ قِطَّةً وَشَجَرَةً وَبَيْتًا.

مُهِمَّة ٦ · Task 6: Draw something on the page. Press Alt+W to turn Write on, then draw with a pen, a finger or the mouse. Or press Alt+K to open a sketch sheet for a diagram.

فِي اللَّيْلِ نَامَ سَالِمٌ مُبَكِّرًا.
